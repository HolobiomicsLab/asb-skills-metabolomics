# LC-HRMS feature-list curation against in silico metabolite predictions

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Curates drug-metabolite features detected by LC-HRMS in human liver S9 incubations by cross-referencing each observed signal (m/z ±5 ppm) against BioTransformer 3.0 predictions, then quantifying how many drugs yield at least one unpredicted metabolite and how rates vary by drug class.

## When to use

Use when per-drug LC-HRMS feature lists (m/z, retention time) from precursor+cosubstrate incubations must be classified as predicted vs unpredicted against a BioTransformer prediction baseline, and summarized per drug class.

## When NOT to use

- The goal is de novo structural elucidation of novel metabolites — this skill only labels signals as predicted/unpredicted against BioTransformer 3.0 and does not identify structures.
- Input is already a curated, class-level summary; re-running the curation would not add information.
- Samples lack the precursor+cosubstrate design (e.g. in vivo or extrahepatic compartments such as intestine or kidney), so the 0-hour vs 24-hour differential criterion cannot be applied.

## Inputs

- Per-drug curated metabolite feature lists (m/z and retention time) for 1114 drugs, from Metabolomics Workbench Study ID ST003991 (project DOI 10.21228/M8W55K)
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite reference table used as the curation baseline
- Drug-class annotations from the Microsource Spectrum Collection metadata and Supplemental Table 1 class breakdown

## Outputs

- Per-metabolite predicted/unpredicted classification for every curated signal of each drug
- Per-class unpredicted-metabolite rates and overall headline rate, written to unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv

## How to apply

Load the per-drug detected-feature lists (m/z and retention time of curated metabolites for each of the 1114 drugs) from Metabolomics Workbench Study ID ST003991 (DOI 10.21228/M8W55K) and the BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite reference table. Retain only features satisfying the paper's identification criteria: detection exclusively in precursor+cosubstrate samples and a ≥10-fold peak-area increase from 0-hour to 24-hour samples (features detected via MS1 and RT comparison in MZmine 2.53 with ADAP chromatogram builder). Classify each curated signal as predicted or unpredicted by matching to BioTransformer 3.0 predictions within ±5 ppm, implemented in R 4.0.3. Compute the fraction of drugs with at least one unpredicted metabolite, then join drug-class annotations (Microsource Spectrum Collection metadata and Supplemental Table 1) to obtain per-class rates, writing the summary to unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv.

## Related tools

- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Provides the in silico predicted-metabolite reference set against which detected LC-HRMS signals are matched within ±5 ppm to label them predicted or unpredicted)
- **MZmine 2.53** (Processes raw LC-HRMS files with the ADAP chromatogram builder to produce the candidate metabolite feature lists that feed the curation step)
- **R 4.0.3** (Performs the ±5 ppm cross-referencing, predicted/unpredicted classification, per-drug-class rate computation, and writes unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv)
- **PharmMet DB** (Provides the experimentally validated curated metabolite lists for the 1114 drugs downloaded from the Metabolomics Workbench project) — https://github.com/ClinicalBiomarkersLaborabory/PharmMet

## Examples

```
Rscript -e 'cur <- subset(met, ppm <= 5); un <- subset(cur, !predicted); write.csv(class_rates, "unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv")'
```

## Evaluation signals

- Computed overall unpredicted rate must equal the reported 997 of 1114 drugs (89%) producing at least one metabolite not predicted by BioTransformer 3.0.
- Per-class rates must reproduce the reported pattern: novel metabolites most frequent for anti-inflammatory, CNS, and antimicrobial classes, with detection rates ranging from ~77% (respiratory drugs) to over 91% (antimicrobial, CNS, other).
- Only signals matching BioTransformer predictions within ±5 ppm are counted as predicted.
- Only features detected exclusively in precursor+cosubstrate samples with a ≥10-fold 0-to-24-hour peak-area increase pass the curation filter.
- Output CSV contains both an overall rate and a per-class breakdown covering the 1114-drug cohort from Supplemental Table 1.

## Limitations

- Reliance on the liver S9 fraction means metabolites formed by enzymes not abundant or active in this fraction, or generated in extrahepatic compartments (e.g. intestine, kidney), are not represented.
- Limited detection of some metabolites may have occurred due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression.
- Novel (unpredicted) metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs remain structurally unelucidated; this curation labels but does not identify them.
