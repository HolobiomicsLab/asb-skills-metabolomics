# r-based-metabolomics-data-processing

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Processing raw LC-HRMS metabolomics output in R 4.0.3 to turn MZmine-derived feature tables into annotated drug-metabolite candidates for PharmMet DB, using differential preincubation/postincubation filtering and BioTransformer cross-referencing.

## When to use

Use when raw Thermo Vanquish UHPLC / Orbitrap ID-X LC-HRMS runs have already been processed into MZmine 2.53 feature tables and you need to annotate candidate drug metabolites by comparing 0-hour preincubation and 24-hour postincubation samples in R.

## When NOT to use

- Input is already a curated list of confirmed metabolite identities or DrugBank entries rather than raw MZmine feature tables requiring differential filtering.
- The samples are from extrahepatic tissues (intestine, kidney, etc.) whose metabolism is not captured by human liver S9 fractions — PharmMet DB is expected to lack such metabolites.
- You need to process raw .RAW mass-spectrometric files end-to-end rather than feature tables already generated by MZmine 2.53.

## Inputs

- MZmine 2.53 feature tables (MS1 m/z, retention time, peak area) exported from Orbitrap ID-X LC-HRMS runs of 0-hour preincubation and 24-hour postincubation human liver S9 samples
- BioTransformer 3.0 in-silico prediction lists for each of the 1114 therapeutic drugs

## Outputs

- Per-feature metabolite annotation table (m/z, RT, 0h/24h presence flags, metabolite status, BioTransformer match status)
- Statistical summaries and data visualizations of metabolite counts and drug-class distributions (e.g. 30,752 oxidized, 6,311 glucuronidated metabolites)

## How to apply

Load the MZmine 2.53 (ADAP chromatogram builder) feature tables exported from the Orbitrap ID-X runs into R 4.0.3, extracting MS1 accurate m/z, retention time, and peak area for each feature. Perform a differential comparison between the 0-hour preincubation and 24-hour postincubation S9 samples, flagging a feature as a candidate drug metabolite when its accurate m/z and RT appear only in the 24-hour sample (precursor drug plus cosubstrates present). Cross-reference detected signals against BioTransformer 3.0 predictions within a ±5 ppm mass-accuracy window, requiring adduct coelution within 3 seconds. Then run statistical analysis and data visualization in R 4.0.3, using GraphPad Prism 6.0 only for supplementary plotting, and write per-feature annotation tables (m/z, RT, presence flags, metabolite status) for downstream PharmMet DB curation. Validation: every tabulated metabolite must satisfy the paper's criteria — detection only in samples containing both precursor and cosubstrate, mass accuracy within ±5 ppm, adduct coelution within 3 seconds, and a time-dependent increase from 0 to 24 hours.

## Related tools

- **R 4.0.3** (Statistical analysis, differential preincubation vs postincubation feature comparison, and data visualization for metabolite annotation)
- **MZmine 2.53** (Upstream raw-file processing with ADAP chromatogram builder to generate the feature tables that R consumes)
- **GraphPad Prism 6.0** (Supplementary statistical plotting alongside R)
- **BioTransformer 3.0** (In-silico metabolite predictions used to curate the ±5 ppm detected mass signals)

## Examples

```
Rscript pharmmet_annotation.R --mzmine-table 0h_features.csv --mzmine-table 24h_features.csv --biotransformer-predictions bt_predictions.csv --ppm-tol 5 --coelution-s 3 --output metabolite_annotation_table.csv
```

## Evaluation signals

- Every feature labelled as a drug metabolite must be absent at 0 hour and present at 24 hours in samples containing both precursor drug and cosubstrate.
- All cross-referenced signals must fall within ±5 ppm of the BioTransformer-predicted exact mass.
- Co-occurring adducts must coelute within 3 seconds of the precursor metabolite peak.
- A time-dependent increase in signal intensity must be demonstrable from 0 to 24 hours for each annotated metabolite.
- Features failing the paper's identification criteria (absence of precursor+cosubstrate, mass error beyond ±5 ppm, missing coelution) must be flagged as non-metabolite or absent in the output table.

## Limitations

- Reliance on the human liver S9 fraction means not all drug-metabolizing activities are present; metabolites generated in extrahepatic compartments (intestine, kidney, etc.) will be missed.
- Limited detection may occur due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression.
- Novel metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs will require considerable additional research for isolation and structural elucidation.
- PharmMet DB is not completely comprehensive despite covering 1114 active therapeutic agents.
