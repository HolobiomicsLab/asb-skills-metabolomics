# confidence-score-ranking-and-rescoring

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

A secondary deep-learning rescoring model trained on MS/MS spectra (e.g., MoNA Q-TOF) assigns confidence scores to candidate molecular formulas generated by FIDDLE's SENIOR-rule post-processing, and candidates are ranked by these scores to produce final top-1/top-5 formula predictions.

## When to use

Use when you have multiple candidate formulas per MS/MS spectrum and need to rank them by a learned confidence score to pick the correct formula, e.g., after FIDDLE prediction plus candidate-formula generation on Q-TOF or Orbitrap spectra.

## When NOT to use

- Spectra with fewer than five peaks, m/z above 1500, or peaks below 50 m/z — these are excluded as unreliable by the standard filters
- When adduct type is unknown and must be predicted itself — adduct type prediction is not part of the current FIDDLE workflow
- Heavily chimeric (co-eluting) spectra, where FIDDLE's performance decreases as spectral mixing increases

## Inputs

- MS/MS spectra in MGF format with title, precursor_mz, precursor_type, and collision_energy fields
- Candidate formula lists per spectrum from FIDDLE breadth-first refinement and SENIOR-rule post-processing
- Instrument-specific spectra with standard filters applied (>=5 peaks, m/z in (0, 1500], peaks below 50 m/z removed, 10 ppm precursor mass tolerance for Q-TOF)

## Outputs

- Per-candidate confidence scores ranking formulas for each spectrum
- Top-1 and top-5 formula predictions per spectrum
- ROC curve / AUC of confidence scores distinguishing correct from incorrect formulas

## How to apply

After FIDDLE's TCN predicts formulas and the breadth-first refinement plus SENIOR-rule post-processing generates candidate formulas aligned to precursor mass, train a secondary rescoring model (train_rescore.py) on the same instrument-specific spectra (e.g., Q-TOF weights fiddle_rescore_qtof.pt) with train/test splits done by molecular canonical SMILES to prevent compound leakage. Score each candidate formula using MS/MS features of the spectrum, then rank candidates by predicted confidence. Evaluate by top-5 coverage of the correct formula and by ROC AUC of confidence scores distinguishing correct from incorrect formulas. Note the v2.0.0 breaking change: the rescore model uses a redesigned Siamese architecture (see CHANGELOG.md).

## Evaluation signals

- Top-5 candidate coverage of the correct formula exceeds 84% on the MoNA Q-TOF evaluation
- ROC AUC of confidence scores distinguishing correct from incorrect formulas reaches ~0.97 on Q-TOF
- Missed-atom histograms of top-1 predictions shift toward zero missed atoms after post-processing plus confidence ranking (Fig. 4a-d)
- Train/test splits are by molecular canonical SMILES, confirming no common compounds leak between train and test sets
- Reported top-1/top-5 accuracies per ablation variant match the paper's values (e.g., full model with augmentation: 9% top-1 all atoms, 26% heavy atoms)

## Limitations

- The rescore model was redesigned as a Siamese architecture in v2.0.0, so results may differ from the original paper's checkpoints
- FDR control via confidence-score thresholds is proposed but not implemented
- No data augmentation was applied for Orbitrap training, and accuracy varies across experimental conditions with less training data
