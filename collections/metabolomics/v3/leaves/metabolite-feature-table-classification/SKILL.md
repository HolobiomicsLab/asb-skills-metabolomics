# metabolite-feature-table-classification

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Classifies curated LC-HRMS drug-metabolite features (m/z, retention time) from human liver S9 incubations as predicted or unpredicted against a BioTransformer 3.0 reference set, then computes the fraction of drugs with ≥1 unpredicted metabolite overall and per therapeutic class.

## When to use

Use when you have per-drug curated LC-HRMS feature lists (m/z + RT) from incubation experiments and a BioTransformer 3.0 prediction table, and need to quantify prediction coverage gaps per drug or drug class.

## When NOT to use

- Your input is already a feature table with predicted/unpredicted labels — re-deriving the classification adds no information.
- You want to evaluate BioTransformer predictions purely in silico without experimentally observed LC-HRMS features from precursor incubations.
- Your metabolites arise from extrahepatic compartments (e.g. intestine, kidney) or enzymes not abundant/active in liver S9 — this classification is only meaningful within the liver S9 incubation system.

## Inputs

- Per-drug curated metabolite feature lists (m/z and retention time for each of the 1114 drugs) from Metabolomics Workbench Study ID ST003991
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite reference table (m/z, formula) used as the curation baseline
- Drug class annotations from the Microsource Spectrum Collection metadata and Supplemental Table 1 (class breakdown of 1114 drugs)

## Outputs

- Per-drug per-metabolite classification table (feature m/z, RT, predicted/unpredicted label)
- Overall unpredicted-metabolite rate (fraction of drugs with ≥1 unpredicted metabolite)
- Per-therapeutic-class unpredicted rates (e.g. unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv written with R 4.0.3)

## How to apply

Download the per-drug detected-feature lists (m/z and RT of curated metabolites for the 1114 drugs) from Metabolomics Workbench Study ID ST003991 (DOI 10.21228/M8W55K) and the BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite reference table used as the curation baseline. Retain only features that satisfy the paper's identification criteria: signals detected exclusively in precursor+cosubstrate samples and showing a ≥10-fold peak-area increase from 0-hour to 24-hour samples, acquired on the dual-column LC-HRMS setup (HILIC/positive ESI and C18/negative ESI on an Orbitrap ID-X). For each observed signal, match to BioTransformer 3.0 predictions within ±5 ppm mass accuracy in R 4.0.3 and label it predicted or unpredicted. Per drug, compute the binary indicator of having at least one unpredicted metabolite; then join drug-class annotations from the Microsource Spectrum Collection metadata and Supplemental Table 1 to aggregate per-class unpredicted rates. Write per-class and overall summaries (e.g. unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv) with R 4.0.3.

## Related tools

- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Provides the predicted-metabolite reference set against which observed signals are matched within ±5 ppm to label each curated feature as predicted or unpredicted)
- **R 4.0.3** (Used to cross-reference detected signals (±5 ppm) with BioTransformer predictions, classify features, join drug-class annotations, and write the summary CSV)
- **Thermo Scientific Orbitrap ID-X Tribrid mass spectrometer** (Acquired the MS1 (60,000 resolution, m/z 100–1000) and MS2 (15,000 resolution, HCD 35%) spectra underlying the feature lists being classified)

## Examples

```
hits <- pred[abs(pred$mz - obs$mz)/obs$mz*1e6 <= 5 & pred$rt == obs$rt, ]; write.csv(unpredicted_summary, 'unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv')
```

## Evaluation signals

- The computed overall unpredicted rate must equal the paper-reported 997 of 1114 drugs (89%).
- Per-class rates must place CNS (~92%) and antimicrobial drugs among the classes with the highest unpredicted rates, matching the article's reported pattern.
- Respiratory drugs must show the lowest detection rate of unpredicted metabolites (~77%).
- Every observed-predicted match must satisfy the ±5 ppm mass-accuracy criterion used in curation.
- Only features detected exclusively in precursor+cosubstrate samples with a ≥10-fold 0-to-24-hour peak-area increase should be counted as curated metabolites.

## Limitations

- Reliance on the liver S9 fraction means metabolites formed by enzymes not abundant or active in this fraction, or generated extrahepatically (e.g. intestine, kidney), are missing.
- Limited detection of some metabolites may have occurred due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression.
- Identification of the novel (unpredicted) metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs will require considerable further research for isolation and structural elucidation.
