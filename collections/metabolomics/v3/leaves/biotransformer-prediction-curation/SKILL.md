# biotransformer-prediction-curation

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Curation of experimentally detected drug-metabolite features by cross-referencing observed mass signals (±5 ppm) against in silico predictions from BioTransformer 3.0, enabling classification of metabolites as predicted vs. novel for PharmMet DB.

## When to use

Use when candidate drug metabolites detected by untargeted LC-HRMS need to be annotated as known/predicted or novel by matching detected precursor masses against a BioTransformer 3.0 prediction database.

## When NOT to use

- Input is raw LC-HRMS vendor data that has not yet been processed into a feature table (run MZmine 2.53 with ADAP chromatogram builder first)
- The goal is de novo structural elucidation of novel metabolites — cross-referencing only classifies detected signals as predicted or novel and does not determine structures
- Metabolites generated extrahepatically (e.g., intestine, kidney) or by enzymes not abundant or active in liver S9 fraction — this workflow cannot detect them

## Inputs

- Detected MS1 metabolite feature table (m/z, retention time, intensity) from MZmine 2.53 processing of 0-hour and 24-hour S9 incubation samples
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted metabolite list for each parent drug (predicted masses/formulas)
- Parent drug list (1114 therapeutic drugs from Microsource Spectrum Collection, DrugBank 5.0 identifiers, Supplemental Table 1 drug-class mapping)

## Outputs

- Annotated metabolite table with per-feature assignment as BioTransformer-predicted or novel metabolite
- Per-drug and per-drug-class counts of predicted vs. detected vs. novel metabolites (e.g., 30,752 oxidized, 6311 glucuronidated metabolites)
- Curated entries compiled into PharmMet DB

## How to apply

After candidate drug metabolite features are detected by comparing MS1 mass and retention times between preincubation (0 hour) and postincubation (24 hour) human liver S9 samples, generate in silico metabolite predictions for each parent drug with BioTransformer 3.0. Cross-reference each observed mass signal against the BioTransformer prediction database using a ±5 ppm mass-accuracy window to assign annotations. Features matching a BioTransformer prediction are labeled as predicted metabolites; features passing the detection criteria (exclusive detection in precursor-plus-cosubstrate samples and ≥10-fold time-dependent signal increase from 0 to 24 hours) but absent from predictions are labeled as novel. Aggregate per drug and per drug class to quantify prediction coverage — e.g., 89% of tested drugs produced at least 1 metabolite not predicted by BioTransformer 3.0, with novel metabolites most frequent in anti-inflammatory, CNS, and antimicrobial classes.

## Related tools

- **BioTransformer 3.0** (In silico prediction of drug metabolites (machine learning plus rule-based algorithms) used as the reference database for cross-referencing detected mass signals)
- **MZmine 2.53** (Processing of LC-HRMS raw files (ADAP chromatogram builder) to produce the feature table whose masses are cross-referenced against BioTransformer predictions)
- **R 4.0.3** (Statistical analysis and data visualization for the differential and curation steps)
- **Pharmaceutical Metabolite Data Base (PharmMet DB)** (Compiled resource into which the curated predicted/novel metabolite annotations are aggregated) — https://github.com/ClinicalBiomarkersLaborabory/PharmMet

## Examples

```
Rscript curate_vs_biotransformer.R --features mzmine_feature_table.csv --predictions biotransformer_predictions.csv --tol_ppm 5 --output curated_metabolites.csv
```

## Evaluation signals

- Mass match tolerance: every predicted-metabolite assignment falls within ±5 ppm of the BioTransformer-predicted mass
- Overall agreement with reported totals: 30,752 oxidized and 6311 glucuronidated metabolites across the 1114-drug library
- Novel-metabolite rate: ~89% of tested drugs show at least 1 metabolite not predicted by BioTransformer 3.0
- Per-class reproduction: detected/predicted counts per drug class match reported figures (e.g., CNS 17,967 of 36,406 detected; cardiovascular 13,089 of 28,302)
- Each annotated metabolite satisfies the detection criteria: exclusive detection in precursor-plus-cosubstrate samples and ≥10-fold 0-to-24-hour signal increase

## Limitations

- Reliance on the liver S9 fraction means not all drug-metabolizing activities are present; metabolites from enzymes not abundant or active in liver S9, or formed extrahepatically, will be missed
- Limited detection of some metabolites may have occurred due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression
- Novel metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs require considerable further research for isolation and structural elucidation; curation assigns masses, not structures
