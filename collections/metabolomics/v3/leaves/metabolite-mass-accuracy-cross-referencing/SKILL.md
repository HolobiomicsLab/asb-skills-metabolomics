# metabolite-mass-accuracy-cross-referencing

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

A curation step that classifies each experimentally detected drug-metabolite feature (paired m/z and retention time from LC-HRMS) as 'predicted' or 'unpredicted' by cross-referencing observed mass signals against in silico predictions (BioTransformer 3.0) within a ±5 ppm mass-accuracy window.

## When to use

Decision support for classifying detected LC-HRMS metabolite features as predicted vs novel by comparing observed m/z values against an in silico metabolite-prediction list within a fixed ppm tolerance.

## When NOT to use

- The feature table has not yet been generated — you still need the 0-hour vs 24-hour differential comparison step to produce candidate metabolite features before cross-referencing can be applied.
- Features lack MS1 m/z values or have only MS2 spectra without accurate-mass data, so a ±5 ppm m/z comparison is not meaningful.
- You are re-annotating literature-curated metabolite identities (e.g. from DrugBank) rather than classifying newly detected mass signals from your own incubation experiment.

## Inputs

- Detected drug-metabolite feature table: paired MS1 m/z and retention time per drug, from 0-hour vs 24-hour human liver S9 LC-HRMS comparison (MZmine 2.53 output)
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite list (m/z values) for the same parent drug

## Outputs

- Per-feature classification table with columns for m/z, RT, predicted/unpredicted label, and matched BioTransformer entry (if any)
- Per-drug counts of predicted vs unpredicted metabolite features
- List of unpredicted (novel) metabolites reported by paired m/z and RT

## How to apply

Take the candidate-metabolite feature table produced by the 0-hour vs 24-hour differential comparison (paired MS1 m/z and RT values per drug) and, for each feature, compute the mass difference between the observed m/z and every predicted metabolite m/z from the BioTransformer 3.0 output for the same parent drug. Retain matches only within ±5 ppm mass accuracy; any feature with at least one such match is labelled 'predicted' and is linked to the matched BioTransformer entry, while features detected only post-incubation with no predicted m/z within ±5 ppm are labelled 'unpredicted' and reported solely by their paired m/z and RT. Rationale: in this PharmMet DB workflow the ±5 ppm window is the stated mass-accuracy criterion for the cross-referencing step, and requiring a post-incubation-only signal ensures the feature is plausibly enzymatically generated by liver S9 rather than an impurity present before incubation. After labelling, summarize per-drug counts of predicted vs unpredicted features (e.g. in R 4.0.3) to support downstream statistics such as the fraction of drugs with at least one novel metabolite.

## Related tools

- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Supplies the in silico predicted-metabolite m/z list that each detected feature is cross-referenced against within ±5 ppm to assign the predicted/unpredicted label.)
- **MZmine 2.53** (Produces the raw detected-feature table (paired m/z and RT) from the LC-HRMS runs, which is the input to the cross-referencing step.)
- **R 4.0.3** (Used for downstream statistical analysis and visualization of per-drug counts of predicted vs unpredicted features after classification.)

## Examples

```
merge(predicted_tbl, bioTransformed_tbl, by = c('drug','mz_pred'), by.x = c('drug','mz_obs'), allow.cartesian = TRUE) |> filter(abs(mz_obs - mz_pred)/mz_pred * 1e6 <= 5)
```

## Evaluation signals

- Every detected feature row carries a non-empty 'predicted' or 'unpredicted' label.
- All features labelled 'predicted' have a matched BioTransformer entry whose m/z is within ±5 ppm of the observed m/z.
- Features labelled 'unpredicted' are present post-incubation (24 h) and absent at 0-hour preincubation, with no predicted m/z match within ±5 ppm.
- Per-drug counts of predicted vs unpredicted features reproduce the reported phenotype (e.g. 89% of tested drugs produced at least one unpredicted metabolite in PharmMet DB).

## Limitations

- The ±5 ppm threshold is a mass-accuracy criterion, not a structural confirmation: labels reflect m/z matching only, and novel (unpredicted) metabolites still require isolation and structural elucidation for full identification.
- Classification quality depends on the completeness of the BioTransformer prediction set for each parent drug; metabolites outside the prediction space are automatically 'unpredicted'.
- Human liver S9 does not capture all drug-metabolizing activities, and limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression may cause metabolites to go undetected, distorting predicted/unpredicted counts.
