# lc-hrms-metabolite-feature-table-processing

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Turns raw dual-column LC-HRMS files from human liver S9 drug incubations into curated drug-metabolite feature tables by processing with MZmine 2.53, comparing 0-hour vs 24-hour MS1/RT data, and cross-referencing signals against BioTransformer 3.0 predictions.

## When to use

Use when raw LC-HRMS files from drug-plus-cosubstrate S9 incubations (0 h and 24 h) must be converted into annotated metabolite feature tables with curated BioTransformer matches and per-drug-class detection counts.

## When NOT to use

- The input is already a processed, annotated feature table — downstream class-aggregation or statistics should run directly instead of re-processing raw files.
- The analysis is a targeted therapeutic-drug-monitoring assay using authenticated reference standards rather than untargeted S9-incubation feature detection.
- The goal is purely in silico metabolite prediction (BioTransformer 3.0 alone) without experimental LC-HRMS validation data.

## Inputs

- Thermo raw LC-HRMS files from Orbitrap ID-X (HILIC+ESI+ and C18+ESI− runs of 0-h and 24-h S9 incubations)
- Supplemental Table 1 drug-class mapping for the 1114 therapeutic drugs
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite lists per parent drug
- Metabolomics Workbench Study ST003991 deposited feature tables (Project DOI 10.21228/M8W55K)

## Outputs

- Curated per-drug metabolite feature tables (m/z, RT, MS1 mass error in ppm, 0→24 h fold change)
- drug_class_metabolite_counts.csv summarizing predicted vs detected metabolites per therapeutic class
- Novel-metabolite lists (features with no BioTransformer 3.0 match)

## How to apply

Process Thermo raw files with MZmine 2.53 using the ADAP chromatogram builder for the dual-column, dual-ESI design (HILIC+positive ESI and C18+negative ESI). Acquire MS1 at 60,000 resolution over m/z 100–1000 and MS2 at 15,000 resolution with HCD 35% on the Orbitrap ID-X. Detect candidate metabolite features by comparing MS1 masses and retention times between preincubation (0 h) and postincubation (24 h) samples, keeping features with mass accuracy within ±5 ppm and a ≥10-fold time-dependent signal increase from 0 to 24 h, exclusive to precursor-plus-cosubstrate samples. Curate observed mass signals by cross-referencing detected signals (±5 ppm) with predictions from the BioTransformer 3.0 data base. Join annotated features to the Supplemental Table 1 drug-class mapping for 1114 therapeutic drugs and aggregate detected-vs-predicted metabolite counts per class.

## Related tools

- **MZmine 2.53** (Processing raw Thermo files with the ADAP chromatogram builder to extract MS1 features and retention times)
- **Thermo Scientific Orbitrap ID-X Tribrid mass spectrometer** (Acquiring MS1 (60,000 resolution, m/z 100–1000) and MS2 (15,000 resolution, HCD 35%) spectra used for feature detection)
- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Providing predicted metabolite lists used to curate observed mass signals within ±5 ppm and classify novel metabolites)
- **R 4.0.3** (Joining annotated features to drug classes (Supplemental Table 1), aggregating counts, and validating reported totals)

## Examples

```
import pandas as pd; ft = pd.read_csv('features_24h.csv'); hits = ft[(ft.mass_error_ppm.abs()<=5) & (ft.fold_change_0to24h>=10) & ft.exclusive_precursor_cosubstrate]
```

## Evaluation signals

- Overall curated counts reproduce the reported 30,752 oxidized and 6311 glucuronidated metabolites.
- Per-class totals match reported ratios, e.g. CNS 17,967 of 36,406 predicted (~49%) and cardiovascular 13,089 of 28,302 (~46%).
- Every retained feature passes all three criteria: mass accuracy within ±5 ppm, ≥10-fold 0→24 h signal increase, and exclusive detection in precursor-plus-cosubstrate samples.
- Novel-metabolite (no BioTransformer 3.0 match) enrichment appears predominantly in anti-inflammatory, CNS, and antimicrobial classes, consistent with the reported finding.
- 89% of tested drugs yield at least 1 metabolite not predicted by BioTransformer 3.0.

## Limitations

- Reliance on the liver S9 fraction means metabolites formed by enzymes not abundant or active in S9, or generated extrahepatically (e.g., intestine, kidney), are expected to be missing.
- Limited detection of some metabolites may occur due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression.
- Novel metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs require considerable research for isolation and structural elucidation before assignment.
