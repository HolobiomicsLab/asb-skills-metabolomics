# bimodal-tissue-enrichment-classification

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Inspect DIMPLE hyperspectral clusters (e.g., Cluster 3–4) in B73 root DESI-MSI data via the GUI to compile two tables: mass signatures bimodally enriched at both meristem and mature tissue (≥16), and Cluster 4 signatures specifically enriched in cortex (>15) or vasculature (>10), annotated via METASPACE, Metlin Classic, and LOTUS to mirror Supplemental Figures 5–7 and Supplemental Table 7.

## When to use

Use when DIMPLE has produced clustered hyperspectral channel assignments along a linear root developmental gradient (e.g., B73 roots in .cdf/.mat workspace files) and you need to separate bimodal developmental signatures from cell-layer-enriched signatures for annotation and tabulation.

## When NOT to use

- Data are from roots without a clear linear developmental axis (e.g., non-root tissues or spiral/branched structures) — DIMPLE was designed to leverage the linear developmental axis of plant roots, so channel assignments along the gradient will not be interpretable.
- Clusters 3–4 have not yet been generated by DIMPLE, or the input is raw unclustered MSI spectra — this skill operates on the clustered hyperspectral channel assignments, not on raw mass spectra.
- The goal is whole-root bulk metabolite quantification rather than spatial enrichment classification — bulk metabolomics masks spatial signatures and is not addressed by this skill.

## Inputs

- DIMPLE clustered hyperspectral channel outputs for B73 roots (CDF files plus MATLAB workspace .mat files, e.g., B73-root11 and B73-root11-workspace.mat)
- DESI-MSI mass signatures (m/z values) assigned to Clusters 3 and 4 along the root developmental gradient

## Outputs

- Signature table of ≥16 bimodal (meristem/mature-tissue enriched) mass signatures from Clusters 3–4
- Signature table of Cluster 4 cortex- and vasculature-enriched m/z values annotated via METASPACE, Metlin Classic, and LOTUS (>15 cortex-enriched; >10 vasculature-enriched)
- Compiled tables mirroring Supplemental Figures 5–7 and Supplemental Table 7

## How to apply

Load the B73 DIMPLE clustered hyperspectral channel outputs (e.g., B73-root11-workspace.mat, B73-root2-5-workspace.mat) and extract channel assignments for Clusters 3 and 4 along the root developmental gradient. For Clusters 3–4, scan the GUI to identify mass signatures whose spatial signal is enriched at both the meristematic region and mature tissue (bimodal pattern) and record them in a signature table — the paper reports at least 16 such signatures. For Cluster 4, separate signatures enriched in the cortex from those enriched in the vasculature based on hyperspectral channel spatial localization, yielding >15 cortex-enriched and >10 vasculature-enriched signatures. Annotate the Cluster 4 cortex- and vasculature-enriched m/z values by searching METASPACE, Metlin Classic, and LOTUS. Compile the two tables so counts and content mirror Supplemental Figures 5–7 and Supplemental Table 7.

## Examples

```
load('B73-root11-workspace.mat'); % inspect Cluster 3–4 hyperspectral channels in GUI; record bimodal (meristem/mature) signatures and Cluster 4 cortex/vasculature m/z, then annotate via METASPACE, Metlin Classic, LOTUS
```

## Evaluation signals

- Bimodal signature table contains at least 16 mass signatures showing enrichment at both meristem and mature tissue in Clusters 3–4.
- Cluster 4 cortex-enriched signature table contains more than 15 mass signatures.
- Cluster 4 vasculature-enriched signature table contains more than 10 mass signatures.
- Compiled tables reproduce the content of Supplemental Figures 5–7 and Supplemental Table 7 in structure and counts.
- Each Cluster 4 cortex/vasculature m/z entry carries annotations from METASPACE, Metlin Classic, and/or LOTUS.

## Limitations

- The majority of metabolites in plants and animals remain unannotated, so annotation coverage for the enriched signatures may be incomplete even after METASPACE, Metlin Classic, and LOTUS searches.
- Spatial resolution of DESI-MSI is only ~50–100 µm, limiting the fidelity of cell-layer (cortex vs. vasculature) assignments.
- Each pixel contains a full mass spectrum with hundreds to thousands of peaks, so untargeted inspection of the GUI requires manual curation and is volume-intensive.
