# Mass signal cross-referencing against in silico predictions

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Curates LC-HRMS-detected drug metabolite signals by matching observed m/z values (±5 ppm) against in silico predicted metabolites from BioTransformer 3.0, classifying each curated feature as predicted or novel. This quantifies the gap between computational prediction and experimentally observed metabolism.

## When to use

Use when an untargeted LC-HRMS feature list (m/z and retention time per sample) must be annotated by matching detected signals against a reference set of in silico predicted metabolites to separate predicted from novel metabolites.

## When NOT to use

- The goal is de novo metabolite structural elucidation — this skill only labels signals as predicted or unpredicted relative to BioTransformer 3.0; novel metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs still require considerable research for isolation and structural elucidation.
- The metabolites of interest arise from extrahepatic compartments or enzymes not abundant/active in liver S9 fraction — PharmMet DB is expected to lack such metabolites, so cross-referencing against it would miss them.
- Signals have not yet passed the candidate-metabolite filter (0-hour vs 24-hour comparison, detection exclusively in precursor+cosubstrate samples, ≥10-fold peak-area increase) — unfiltered features would inflate novel-metabolite counts.

## Inputs

- Per-drug detected-feature lists (m/z and retention time of curated metabolites for each of the 1114 drugs) from Metabolomics Workbench Study ID ST003991
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite reference table (curation baseline)
- Drug class annotations from the Microsource Spectrum Collection metadata and Supplemental Table 1

## Outputs

- Per-metabolite predicted/unpredicted classification for each of the 1114 drugs
- Overall fraction of drugs with at least one unpredicted metabolite (e.g. 997/1114 = 89%)
- Per-therapeutic-class novel-metabolite detection rates
- unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv

## How to apply

Download the per-drug detected-feature lists (m/z and retention time of curated metabolites for each of the 1114 drugs) from Metabolomics Workbench Study ID ST003991 via DOI 10.21228/M8W55K, retaining only features that satisfy the identification criteria (detection exclusively in precursor+cosubstrate samples and a ≥10-fold 0-to-24-hour peak-area increase). Load the BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite reference table and match each observed signal to predictions within ±5 ppm mass accuracy in R 4.0.3. For each drug, classify every curated metabolite as predicted or unpredicted, then compute the fraction of drugs with at least one unpredicted metabolite (expected ~997/1114 = 89%). Join drug class annotations from the Microsource Spectrum Collection metadata and Supplemental Table 1 to compute per-class novel-metabolite rates, and write the summary to unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv.

## Related tools

- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Provides the in silico predicted-metabolite reference set used as the curation baseline for ±5 ppm cross-referencing of detected mass signals)
- **R 4.0.3** (Environment for matching detected signals to BioTransformer predictions, classifying metabolites, and computing per-class novel-metabolite rates)
- **MZmine 2.53** (Upstream processing (with ADAP chromatogram builder) that produced the raw feature lists fed into the cross-referencing step)

## Examples

```
Rscript classify_unpredicted_metabolites.R --features ST003991_per_drug_features.csv --biotransformer biotransformer3_predictions.csv --ppm 5 --classes supplemental_table1.csv --out unpredicted_metabolite_summary.csv
```

## Evaluation signals

- The computed overall unpredicted rate must match the paper-reported 997/1114 (89%) figure.
- Per-class ordering must place CNS and antimicrobial classes among the highest novel-metabolite rates (e.g. ~92% for CNS drugs) and respiratory among the lowest (~77%), as reported in the article.
- Every matched signal must fall within ±5 ppm mass accuracy of a BioTransformer 3.0 prediction.
- Only features satisfying the identification criteria (detection exclusively in precursor+cosubstrate samples; ≥10-fold 0-to-24-hour peak-area increase) should be classified.
- The output CSV must cover all 1114 drugs and join cleanly to the Supplemental Table 1 class breakdown.

## Limitations

- Reliance upon the liver S9 fraction for biotransformation means not all drug-metabolizing activities are present in this fraction.
- PharmMet DB is expected to lack metabolites formed by enzymes not abundant or active in the liver S9 fraction, or generated in extrahepatic compartments (e.g. intestine, kidney).
- Limited detection of some metabolites may have occurred due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression.
- The identification of novel metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs will require considerable research for isolation and structural elucidation.
