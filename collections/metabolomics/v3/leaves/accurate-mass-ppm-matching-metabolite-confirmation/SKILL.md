# accurate-mass-ppm-matching-metabolite-confirmation

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Confirms candidate drug metabolite signals by matching accurate MS1 m/z within a ±5 ppm mass window against BioTransformer 3.0 predicted metabolites and against the 24-hour postincubation human liver S9 sample, ensuring that each annotated metabolite in PharmMet DB rests on a mass-accurate, time-differentiated detection rather than an unvalidated in-silico prediction.

## When to use

Use when LC-HRMS feature tables (MS1 m/z, retention time, peak area) from preincubation (0 h) and postincubation (24 h) human liver S9 incubations are available and candidate metabolites must be confirmed by accurate-mass (±5 ppm) matching against BioTransformer 3.0 predictions before entry into a metabolite database.

## When NOT to use

- Input is a raw mzML/Thermo RAW file rather than an MZmine 2.53 feature table — peak detection must be run first.
- Samples lack a matched 0-hour preincubation control, so the temporal differential criterion (present at 24 h, absent at 0 h) cannot be evaluated.
- The target metabolite is generated by extrahepatic or S9-absent enzymes (e.g. intestinal or renal biotransformation), which the article notes PharmMet DB is expected to miss.

## Inputs

- MZmine 2.53 (ADAP chromatogram builder) feature tables with MS1 accurate m/z, retention time, and peak area per feature, derived from raw Orbitrap ID-X files on Metabolomics Workbench Study ST003991
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted metabolite list for each parent drug, containing predicted monoisotopic masses/formulas
- Parent drug list (1114 therapeutic drugs from Microsource Spectrum Collection, DrugBank 5.0, and PubMed), with known monoisotopic masses

## Outputs

- Per-drug table of confirmed metabolite features: observed accurate m/z (within ±5 ppm of a BioTransformer 3.0 prediction), retention time, presence flag at 0 h vs 24 h, and BioTransformer-matched metabolite identity
- Count and class breakdown of confirmed vs BioTransformer-unpredicted metabolites per drug (e.g. 89% of tested drugs producing ≥1 unpredicted metabolite)

## How to apply

Load the MZmine 2.53 (ADAP chromatogram builder) feature tables generated from Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC–Orbitrap ID-X Tribrid acquisition (MS1 at 60,000 resolution, m/z 100–1000; MS2 at 15,000 resolution, HCD 35%). Extract per-feature accurate m/z and retention time for the 0-hour preincubation and 24-hour postincubation S9 samples. Flag a feature as a candidate drug metabolite when its MS1 accurate m/z and RT are present in the 24-hour postincubation sample and absent at 0 hour (the differential criterion), then match that m/z to a BioTransformer 3.0 predicted metabolite formula using a ±5 ppm mass-accuracy window. Only features passing both the temporal differential test and the ±5 ppm BioTransformer cross-reference are accepted as experimentally validated entries in the PharmMet DB output table; R 4.0.3 is used for the matching and tabulation.

## Related tools

- **MZmine 2.53** (Produces the ADAP chromatogram builder feature tables whose MS1 m/z and RT values are the substrate for the ±5 ppm matching step.)
- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Supplies the predicted metabolite masses/formulas against which the observed m/z are cross-referenced within the ±5 ppm window.)
- **R 4.0.3** (Used for statistical analysis and data visualization of the mass-matching results and metabolite annotation tables.)
- **Thermo Scientific Orbitrap ID-X Tribrid mass spectrometer** (Acquires the high-resolution MS1 spectra whose accurate m/z make the ±5 ppm matching feasible.)

## Examples

```
Rscript confirm_metabolites.R --features mzmine_features_ST003991.csv --bio_transformer biotransformer_predictions.csv --ppm 5 --output metabolite_confirmation_table.csv
```

## Evaluation signals

- Every annotated metabolite feature must show a monoisotopic m/z deviation within ±5 ppm of its matched BioTransformer 3.0 predicted metabolite.
- Every annotated metabolite must be detected in the 24-hour postincubation sample and absent from the 0-hour preincubation sample (temporal differential criterion).
- Cross-check the annotation table against Metabolomics Workbench Study ST003991 data to confirm reproducibility of m/z and RT values.
- For each drug, verify that at least 2 metabolites are annotated when required for enzyme-based chemical identification, as stated in the article's design rationale.
- Confirm the output schema includes m/z, RT, and the 0 h vs 24 h presence flags for each feature.

## Limitations

- Human liver S9 fraction lacks some drug-metabolizing activities, so metabolites formed by absent enzymes will not be detected or matched.
- PharmMet DB is expected to lack metabolites generated in extrahepatic compartments (e.g. intestine, kidney).
- Limited detection of some metabolites may occur due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression, producing false negatives in the 24 h sample that never reach the matching step.
- The matching only validates mass accuracy, not structure — novel metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs will require further isolation and structural elucidation.
