# lc-hrms-msms-spectrum-matching

## Summary

Validates MS1 molecular-formula annotations from IDSL.UFA by matching each peak's MS/MS fragmentation spectrum against a reference spectral library (e.g. NIST 2020) to identify compounds whose formula is confirmed, then reports concordance statistics such as the fraction of MS/MS-confirmed hits with a median rank ≤ 2.

## When to use

Use when you have IDSL.UFA MS1 formula annotations for an aligned LC/HRMS peak table plus tandem-MS (MS2) spectra and a reference library, and you need to confirm which formula assignments are supported by fragmentation data.

## When NOT to use

- When the peak table already contains MS/MS-confirmed structural annotations and only needs re-annotation — the MS1 isotopic-profile matching step (IDSL.UFA) is redundant and the MS/MS confirmation step adds no information.
- When input spectra are MS1-only with no MS/MS scans available — the concordance statistic cannot be computed because the skill relies on matching fragmentation spectra against a reference library.
- When the study is a targeted assay with pre-identified compounds rather than untargeted LC/HRMS peaks — IDSL.UFA formula annotation and MS/MS confirmation are designed for database-independent annotation of unknown peaks in population-scale exposomics/metabolomics data.

## Inputs

- Aligned annotated peak table (individual and aligned peak lists from IDSL.IPA, annotated with molecular formula hits by IDSL.UFA)
- Raw LC/HRMS data files with MS/MS spectra (mzXML, mzML, netCDF)
- Reference tandem mass spectral library (e.g. NIST 2020 MS/MS library)
- IPDB (.Rdata) theoretical isotopic-profile database used by IDSL.UFA (e.g. IDSL.ExposomeDB IPDB)

## Outputs

- Count and percentage table of MS/MS-confirmed hits binned by median rank (e.g. median_rank_distribution.csv)
- Set of peaks whose assigned molecular formula is supported by MS/MS library matching

## How to apply

Start from the aligned annotated peak table and individual peak lists generated by IDSL.IPA and annotated with molecular formulas by IDSL.UFA (MS1-only isotopic-profile matching). For each peak in the aligned table, extract its MS/MS spectrum from the raw mzML/mzXML data and match it against a reference library (e.g. the NIST 2020 MS/MS library) to identify the compound whose formula is confirmed. Then retrieve the molecular formula hits that IDSL.UFA assigned to that MS/MS-confirmed compound and compute the median rank of the confirmed formula hit across the individual samples in the aligned table. Bin the confirmed hits by median rank and report the proportion falling at or below a chosen rank threshold (e.g. median rank ≤ 2 or the IDSL.UFA-recommended reporting threshold of median rank ≤ 5). The rationale is that MS1 isotopic-profile matching alone can be ambiguous, so MS2-based library confirmation serves as an orthogonal check on whether the top-ranked formula hits correspond to the true underlying compound.

## Evaluation signals

- The proportion of MS/MS-confirmed hits with a median rank ≤ 2 should be in the ballpark reported for ST002044 (78.75% of 73 confirmed hits).
- Every MS/MS-confirmed peak must be traceable back to a formula hit in the IDSL.UFA-annotated aligned peak table; no orphan confirmations.
- Median ranks are computed across individual samples (not just the aligned table), and the distribution should be discrete and integer-valued where sample counts are small.
- The output CSV (median_rank_distribution.csv) should contain both counts and percentages per median-rank bin.
- Cross-check that the number of MS/MS-confirmed hits (e.g. 73 in ST002044) is a subset of the total unique molecular formulas reported at the aligned-table level.

## Limitations

- The skill only validates the subset of peaks with library-matched MS/MS spectra; many exposomics-relevant compounds (e.g. chlorinated perfluorotriether alcohols) lack reference spectra and remain unconfirmed.
- The NIST 2020 library is a 'database dependent' reference; compounds poorly represented in such libraries will be missed, mirroring the IDSL.UFA caveat about database-dependent formula-annotation tools.
- Median-rank concordance is a population-level statistic and does not guarantee the correct formula for any individual peak with a high median rank.
- Accuracy depends on IDSL.IPA's peak picking and recursive mass correction; IDSL.IPA claims to outperform MZmine 2, xcms, and MS-DIAL, but errors there propagate to the ranks being validated.
