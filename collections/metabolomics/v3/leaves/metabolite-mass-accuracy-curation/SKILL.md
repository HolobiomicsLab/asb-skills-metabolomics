# metabolite-mass-accuracy-curation

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Curation step for candidate drug metabolite mass signals from LC-HRMS differential analysis (0-h vs 24-h human liver S9 incubation), where each detected feature is cross-referenced against BioTransformer 3.0 in silico predictions using a ±5 ppm mass-accuracy window to label metabolites as predicted or novel.

## When to use

Use when postincubation (24-h) drug metabolite features detected by LC-HRMS differential analysis need to be curated and classified against in silico predictions (e.g., BioTransformer 3.0) using a defined mass-accuracy tolerance.

## When NOT to use

- The input is already a curated metabolite list with confirmed structures (e.g., extracted from DrugBank or literature) rather than raw differential MS1 features needing prediction-based curation.
- The experiment lacks a paired preincubation (0-hour) control, so the 0-h vs 24-h differential comparison used to define candidate metabolite features cannot be performed.
- Detection relies on a single-stage low-resolution MS assay without accurate-mass capability, making the ±5 ppm cross-referencing against BioTransformer 3.0 predictions unfeasible.

## Inputs

- MZmine 2.53–processed MS1 feature list (m/z, retention time, intensity) from 0-hour preincubation and 24-hour postincubation S9 samples
- BioTransformer 3.0–predicted metabolite list for each incubated therapeutic drug (predicted molecular masses/formulae)
- Incubated drug identity / parent compound mass (from DrugBank 5.0–derived drug list)

## Outputs

- Curated metabolite annotation table labeling each 24-h feature as 'predicted by BioTransformer 3.0' or 'not predicted (novel)' within ±5 ppm
- Per-drug counts of predicted vs novel metabolites feeding PharmMet DB and summary statistics (e.g., 59,869/130,464 = 45.89% detected)

## How to apply

After MZmine 2.53 (ADAP chromatogram builder) processing of the Orbitrap ID-X raw files, compare mass spectral data (MS1) and retention times between preincubation (0 hour) and postincubation (24 hour) samples to isolate candidate drug metabolite features attributable to S9-mediated biotransformation rather than the parent drug. For each candidate feature, cross-reference the accurate mass of the detected signal against the metabolites predicted in silico by BioTransformer 3.0 for the incubated drug, accepting a match only when the measured m/z falls within ±5 ppm of the predicted value. Metabolites that match a BioTransformer 3.0 prediction within the ±5 ppm window are labeled as predicted; those with no prediction inside the window are labeled as novel (not predicted), the class of metabolites that appeared for 89% of the 1114 tested drugs. Record the classification per drug and metabolite so downstream steps can compute detection counts (e.g., 59,869 of 130,464 predicted metabolites detected) and novelty rates by drug class (anti-inflammatory, CNS, antimicrobial). Retention-time consistency across the dual-column/dual-ESI setup (HILIC-positive ESI, C18-negative ESI) should be preserved when matching, because RT is part of the differential-detection evidence along with MS1 mass.

## Related tools

- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Supplies the in silico predicted metabolite set against which detected mass signals are cross-referenced at ±5 ppm for curation)
- **MZmine 2.53** (Processes raw LC-HRMS files (ADAP chromatogram builder) to produce the MS1 feature lists whose 0-h vs 24-h differences define candidate metabolite signals)
- **Thermo Scientific Orbitrap ID-X Tribrid mass spectrometer** (Acquires the high-resolution MS1 spectra (60,000 resolution, m/z 100–1000) whose accurate masses enable ±5 ppm matching to predictions)
- **R 4.0.3** (Used for statistical analysis and data visualization of curated metabolite counts and novelty rates)

## Examples

```
Rscript curate_metabolites.R --features mzm_features_0h_vs_24h.csv --predictions biotransformer3.0_metabolites.csv --ppm 5 --out curated_metabolites.csv
```

## Evaluation signals

- Every accepted 'predicted' metabolite call has an absolute mass error ≤ 5 ppm versus its BioTransformer 3.0 prediction.
- No candidate feature appears in the 0-hour preincubation sample that is not present in the 24-hour postincubation sample (0-h vs 24-h differential logic holds).
- Counts reconcile with reported totals: 130,464 predicted metabolites across 1114 drugs, of which 59,869 (45.89%) were detected.
- Novelty classification reproduces the reported 89% of tested drugs producing at least one metabolite not predicted by BioTransformer 3.0.
- Each curated metabolite retains its measured m/z and retention time from the MZmine feature list, allowing re-matching against predictions.

## Limitations

- A limitation of the design was reliance upon the liver S9 fraction for biotransformation; not all drug-metabolizing activities are present in this fraction.
- PharmMet DB is expected to lack metabolites formed by enzymes that are not abundant or active in the liver S9 fraction or that are generated in extrahepatic compartments (eg, intestine, kidney).
- Limited detection of some metabolites may have occurred due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression.
- The identification of novel metabolites from nearly 1000 drugs will require considerable research for isolation and structural elucidation.
