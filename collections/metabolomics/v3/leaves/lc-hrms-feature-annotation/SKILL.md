# lc-hrms-feature-annotation

> **License: noncommercial** — confirm your use is a permitted (noncommercial) purpose before applying; commercial use requires a separate license (see `metadata.tool_license`). <!-- asb-license-banner -->
## Summary

Annotates LC-HRMS mass-spectral features (m/z and retention time pairs) from human liver S9 incubations of therapeutic drugs by differentially comparing 0-hour preincubation and 24-hour postincubation samples, then labelling each detected signal as predicted or unpredicted against BioTransformer 3.0 within a ±5 ppm mass tolerance.

## When to use

Use when raw LC-HRMS files from paired 0-hour and 24-hour drug incubations must be converted into an annotated metabolite feature table with predicted/unpredicted labels.

## When NOT to use

- Input is already an annotated feature table with predicted/unpredicted labels; re-annotating would not add information
- Samples lack paired 0-hour and 24-hour incubations, so the differential preincubation/postincubation comparison cannot be performed
- Analysis targets MS2-based structural identification or novel-metabolite isolation/structural elucidation rather than m/z-and-RT-level annotation; the article notes such identification 'will require considerable research'

## Inputs

- Raw LC-HRMS data files from paired 0-hour (preincubation) and 24-hour (postincubation) drug incubations, deposited at Metabolomics Workbench Study ID ST003991 (Project DOI 10.21228/M8W55K)
- BioTransformer 3.0 predicted-metabolite lists (predicted m/z values) for each parent drug
- MS1 spectral data acquired at 60,000 resolution with m/z scan range 100–1000, and MS2 spectra at 15,000 resolution with HCD 35%

## Outputs

- Tab-separated peak table (m/z, retention time, peak intensities per sample) exported from MZmine 2.53
- Per-drug annotated feature table with columns for m/z, RT, predicted/unpredicted label, and matched BioTransformer entry (if any)
- Per-drug counts of predicted and unpredicted features

## How to apply

Import raw LC-HRMS data (from the Thermo Scientific Vanquish UHPLC coupled to an Orbitrap ID-X Tribrid mass spectrometer) into MZmine 2.53 and build chromatograms with the ADAP chromatogram builder, processing each sample independently to extract MS1 m/z and retention-time features. Detect candidate drug metabolite features by comparing MS1 and RT between preincubation (0 hour) and postincubation (24 hour) samples, retaining signals present postincubation but absent preincubation. Curation of observed mass signals is performed by cross-referencing the detected signals (±5ppm) with predictions from the BioTransformer 3.0 data base: a match within the tolerance labels the feature 'predicted', while a postincubation-only feature with no matching predicted m/z within ±5 ppm is labelled 'unpredicted' (novel, reported by paired m/z and RT). Assemble a per-drug feature table with columns for m/z, RT, predicted/unpredicted label, and matched BioTransformer entry, then summarize per-drug counts (e.g. with R 4.0.3) to support downstream statistics such as the fraction of drugs with at least one novel metabolite.

## Related tools

- **MZmine 2.53** (Processes raw LC-HRMS files into m/z and RT feature lists using the ADAP chromatogram builder, the upstream step of this annotation skill)
- **BioTransformer 3.0** (Provides the predicted metabolite m/z values against which detected features are cross-referenced at ±5 ppm to assign predicted/unpredicted labels)
- **R 4.0.3** (Performs statistical analysis and data visualization on the resulting per-drug feature counts)
- **Thermo Scientific Orbitrap ID-X Tribrid mass spectrometer** (Instrument that acquired the MS1 and MS2 spectra feeding this annotation workflow)

## Examples

```
Rscript annotate_features.R --peak_table mzmine_peak_table.tsv --biotransformer biotransformer_predictions.tsv --ppm 5
```

## Evaluation signals

- Every detected feature row carries a non-empty 'predicted' or 'unpredicted' label
- All features labelled 'predicted' satisfy the ±5 ppm mass-accuracy criterion against a BioTransformer 3.0 m/z
- Each unpredicted (novel) feature is reported as a paired m/z and RT value and is detectable only postincubation (absent at 0 hour)
- The exported peak table contains m/z and RT columns with entries for both the 0-hour and 24-hour samples of each drug incubation
- The fraction of drugs with at least one unpredicted metabolite is consistent with the reported 89% of tested drugs producing at least 1 metabolite not predicted on BioTransformer 3.0

## Limitations

- Reliance on the liver S9 fraction for biotransformation means not all drug-metabolizing activities are present, so PharmMet DB is expected to lack metabolites formed by enzymes not abundant or active in the liver S9 fraction or generated in extrahepatic compartments (e.g. intestine, kidney)
- Limited detection of some metabolites may have occurred due to limited cofactor availability or ionization suppression
- Novel metabolites are reported only as paired m/z and RT values; their identification and structural elucidation 'will require considerable research'
